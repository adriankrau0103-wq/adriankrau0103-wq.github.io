Island Poster

Island Poster is a private, single-user tool. Its owner creates short animated island story videos and the tool publishes them to the owner's own TikTok account on a schedule. It is not offered to the public.

Connect with TikTok

How it works

  1. Login Kit (user.info.basic): the owner authorizes the tool once; the display name and avatar are shown here to confirm which account is connected.
  2. Content Posting API – Direct Post (video.publish): the scheduler renders the next episode (MP4, 1080×1920), uploads it with FILE_UPLOAD and publishes it to the owner's account with title, privacy level and the AI-generated label.
  3. Content Posting API – Upload (video.upload): optional fallback that sends the video to the owner's TikTok inbox as a draft, so the owner can review and post it in the TikTok app.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

  • Data collected: After the owner authorizes the app with TikTok, the app stores an access token, a refresh token and the basic profile information of the owner's own TikTok account (open ID, display name, avatar). No data about other TikTok users is collected.
  • Purpose: The tokens are used exclusively to upload and publish the owner's videos and to read the owner's own video statistics and comments.
  • Storage: Data is stored in the owner's private workspace and is not shared with, sold to or transferred to any third party.
  • Retention: Tokens are kept until the owner revokes the app's access in TikTok's settings or deletes them, whichever comes first.
  • Contact: Questions about this policy can be sent to the owner at the e-mail address registered with the TikTok developer account.
